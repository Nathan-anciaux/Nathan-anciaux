03 · Projects

Projects.

2026 · Python · 3 months

Simulated annealing for the travelling salesman problem

A stochastic algorithm: Metropolis–Hastings on a discrete space, with a temperature schedule and a stopping criterion; computing time related to a complexity measured in operations.

2,783 instances of the Kaggle challenge solved in 79 s on 20 cores (speed-up × 19); tours 17% shorter than the nearest-neighbour heuristic and on average 1% shorter than 2-opt.

The background of this page runs the same method, live: scrolling down cools the system.

  • Python
  • Monte-Carlo
  • Metropolis–Hastings
  • Optimisation
  • Parallel computing

2025 · C · 3 months

Finite automata in C

An automata library inside a supplied interactive shell: from regular expressions to automata and back, built on bit-packed Boolean arrays and graph structures.

Algorithms: Thompson's construction, subset determinisation, Brzozowski minimisation, product intersection, and Brzozowski–McCluskey, from an automaton back to a regular expression.

  • C
  • Automata theory
  • Regular expressions
  • Git

2025 · C · 3 months

Jewels & Algorithms

The engine of a Candy Crush-style game: stable board generation, swaps, chain reactions under four-way gravity, scoring, levels, saves and three simple AIs. Then a puzzle solver that searches the graph of board configurations by BFS and DFS.

An 8×8 board has over 10⁵⁰ configurations: the solver never lists them, and stores the boards already visited in a hash table.

  • C
  • BFS · DFS
  • Hash tables

2026 · Python · Dash · 6 months

Stock-price prediction app

A Python and Dash web app on a Kaggle dataset of listed car manufacturers: candlestick charts, volume histograms, opening and closing prices, a pie chart, and a random-forest forecast of the next opening price. Object-oriented code, with classes split along a model–view–controller architecture.

  • Python
  • Dash
  • Random forest
  • Data visualisation
  • OOP

2025–2026 · C · 8 months

Tents & Trees

A C library for the Tents puzzle, built over two semesters: toric grids, big trees that need two tents, undo and redo, saves, and a generator of solvable grids. Playable in a terminal, an SDL2 app or the browser, through WebAssembly. Over forty unit tests.

Backtracking solver: it places tents tree by tree and cuts any branch where two tents touch or a row or column overflows.

  • C
  • Backtracking
  • SDL2
  • WebAssembly

2025 · R · Year 1 · 6 months

Air quality across the world

A statistical study of open air-quality data from OpenAQ, about 61,000 measurements: PM2.5, PM10 and NO₂ sorted by country and graded against health thresholds, mapped sensor by sensor on interactive Leaflet maps, averaged per pollutant, and cross-checked in correlation matrices for France, Norway and India. Ended with an oral defence.

Conclusion: not every country breathes the same air, and some exceed the WHO's critical thresholds.

  • R
  • Leaflet
  • Correlation matrices
  • Data visualisation
